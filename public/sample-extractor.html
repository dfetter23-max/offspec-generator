🧪 Install Sample # Grabber

Stop hunting for sample numbers during 2nd Sets.

You know the drill — you've got the LSR open, you need to pull sample numbers for ZLO01, and you're scanning through duplicates trying not to miss the odd one out. It's tedious and it's easy to slip up.

This tool does it for you in one click. With the LSR open in Chrome, hit the bookmarklet and it instantly grabs every sample number on the page, strips out the duplicates, and copies the clean list straight to your clipboard. All that's left is to paste into the batch section of ZLO01 in Lab.

It takes 30 seconds to install and saves you time on every single load. Seriously — give it a shot.

1
Drag this button to your Chrome bookmarks bar:
🧪 Sample # Grabber
↑ Click and drag the blue button up to your bookmarks bar and drop it there.
If you don't see the bookmarks bar, press Ctrl+Shift+B to show it.
2
Open an SAP LSR PDF in Chrome.
3
Click Sample # Grabber in your bookmarks bar.
It scans the PDF, finds all sample numbers, removes duplicates, and shows them on screen already selected.
4
Press Ctrl+C to copy, then paste (Ctrl+V) into the batch section of ZLO01 — done.
Requirements:
• Must be run on an SAP PDF open in Chrome
• Only needs to be installed once — it stays in your bookmarks bar
• Finds sample numbers matching the pattern S + 9 digits (e.g. S000123456)
