Drum Photo

iPhone setup — do this once on each phone

Scan with the iPhone Camera

or type in Safari:
ec-receiving.web.app/drum-photo

  1. 1
    Scan the code
    Open the Camera, point it at the code, and tap the link that pops up. It opens in Safari.
  2. 2
    Tap Share
    The square with the up arrow at the bottom of Safari. Don't see it? Tap ••• first, then Share.
  3. 3
    Add to Home Screen
    Scroll down the list, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. ✓
    Done
    From now on, just tap the Drum Photo icon on the home screen.

Using it
