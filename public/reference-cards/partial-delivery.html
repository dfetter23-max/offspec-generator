Fixing a Partially Delivered Drum

Sometimes an order line only partly delivers: SAP shows an odd Delivered qty instead of the full drum count, and the drum can't be fully delivered or billed. This guide walks through the fix with a simple example.

1Find the problem on the Schedule lines tab

Open the sales order, double-click the line item, and go to the Schedule lines tab. Order Quantity is 2, but Delivered qty shows 1.050.

2Work out the difference

Subtract the delivered quantity from the total number of drums.

2 (total drums) − 1.050 (delivered) = 0.950

3Split the schedule line

  1. A In the first row, type 0.950 in Order quantity and press Enter. Keep pressing Enter until 0.950 shows in all three columns (Order, Rounded, Confirmed).
  2. B In the next empty row, type 1.050 in Order quantity and enter the same date as row 1.
  3. Press Enter until 1.050 shows in all three columns.

The two rows must add back up to the total: 0.950 + 1.050 = 2.

4Deliver and correct the quantity

Click Sales document → Deliver at the top. On the delivery screen that opens, change Deliv. Qty to the total number of drums, 2, and Save. The drum is now fully delivered.

Easier cases: check these first

Quick fixConfirmed Qty is blank

If the Confirmed Qty column is just missing the number, type it in (2) and deliver. No split needed.

Quick fixFuture delivery date

If the schedule line has a date in the future, change it to today's date and deliver.
