Outliers & cross-dataset insights

Patterns worth investigating

Dual paperwork + material offenders

These producers appear in both the paperwork dataset (Jan–Apr 2026) and the material quality dataset (Jan 2025–Apr 2026). They need holistic corrective actions covering both documentation and waste quality.

ProducerPaperworkMaterialCombinedMat. revenue

Newington backlog concentration

SC Newington has 182 notifications per $100K revenue — 2.2× the rate of East Chicago — and carries 18 of 36 open items (50% of the backlog). This is almost entirely driven by Boston Green Fuel. Fixing BGF would normalize Newington's rate overnight.

EC efficiency under load

SC East Chicago handles 43% of all paperwork notifications but runs at 82 per $100K revenue with a 5.4-day average resolution — the best efficiency of any high-volume center. Whatever process EC is running should be studied and replicated.

Monthly trend: January surge

January 2026 had 112 notifications — more than Feb (55), Mar (69), and Apr (29) combined. This could be a post-holiday backlog, a year-start data cleanup push, or seasonal volume. Worth monitoring in Jan 2027.
