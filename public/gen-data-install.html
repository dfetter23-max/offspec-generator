Gen-Data Off-Spec

v2 full-page tool — not a bookmarklet

An all-in-one off-spec generator. Fill the manifest header, tap the issue(s), and it writes a finished OFFSPEC .xlsx into your real template (logo, borders, and all three tabs preserved) — plus a built-in Notification Builder for the matching customer/CSR message. Unlike the LSR Grabber, this isn't a javascript: bookmarklet you drag — it's a normal page you save to your bookmarks bar so it's one click away.

What it does

writes your template Generates the OFFSPEC .xlsx straight into your real EC template — logo, borders, and all three tabs are preserved, so the output is ready to send, not a blank rebuild. tap-to-build issues Tap one or more issue buttons and the Miscellaneous text auto-fills with the right wording. Everything stays editable, and a live preview of the off-spec updates as you type. manifest header One place for Sales Order, Load #, received/current dates, manifest #, generator name, and optional generator site / mailing numbers. notification builder A second screen confirms details per issue and builds the matching notification, with a one-click Copy for the sales order number. editable mappings Adjust the Section code, Waste Stream #, and amend phrase behind each issue button, or reset to defaults — no code changes needed. EC / WI / TN Switch facility and initials in the header; the generated paperwork follows the selected site.

How to use

  1. Click your Gen-Data Off-Spec bookmark to open the tool.
  2. Pick the Facility and confirm your Initials in the top bar.
  3. Fill the Manifest & Header fields (Sales Order, Load #, dates, manifest, generator).
  4. Tap the issue button(s). The Miscellaneous text auto-fills — edit it if needed and watch the live preview.
  5. Click GENERATE OFFSPEC to download the finished .xlsx in your real template.
  6. Hit the ▼ arrow to drop into the Notification Builder, confirm details, and copy the sales order / notification text.
