Outliers & emerging trends

Patterns worth investigating — Jan 2025 through Apr 2026

SC Pittsburgh haz code bottleneck

Haz Code Issues average 39.0 days at Pittsburgh vs. 10.3 days at East Chicago — nearly 4× slower on the identical issue type. Chemical Analysis shows a similar gap (13.6 vs. 6.4 days). This is a center-level process or staffing issue, not a producer mix problem.

Seasonal resolution pattern

August (15.3d), October (12.9d), November (15.2d), and December (13.3d) consistently spike. Summer coverage and holiday staffing are the likely drivers.

Axalta trending worse while others improve

Axalta (both SAP entries) doubled from 15 notifications in H1 2025 to 31 in Q4+Q1 2026. Meanwhile, prior top offenders improved: NSA −40, Univar Clearfield −38, SUNY Buffalo −9.

Dual paperwork + material offenders

These producers appear in both datasets. They need holistic corrective actions, not two separate conversations.

Profile issues Q4 2025 spike

Profile Issues resolution jumped from 3.6 days (Q2 2025) to 18.2 days (Q4 2025) — a 5× increase, concentrated in university lab pack accounts. Q1 2026 partially recovered to 9.3 days.
